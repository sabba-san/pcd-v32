Follow these steps to get the most out of the platform.

    Create Your Account & Log In

Frequently Asked Questions

What is DLP Advisor? expand_more
DLP Advisor is a platform that helps homeowners, developers, and lawyers manage defect liability period (DLP) claims. It provides 3D scan visualization, AI-powered defect analysis, evidence management, and automated tribunal report generation.
How do I report a defect? expand_more
Navigate to Lidar Scans, select your project, and open the 3D visualizer. Click directly on the 3D model to pin a defect, then fill in the defect type, severity, and description. You can also upload photos as evidence.
How does the AI report generation work? expand_more
The AI analyzes all uploaded evidence, defect descriptions, and historical data to produce a comprehensive tribunal-ready report. Lawyers can review, verify, and override risk assessments before finalizing. The report can be exported as a PDF.
Can I use DLP Advisor on my phone? expand_more
Yes. The platform is fully responsive and works on mobile devices. The 3D visualizer is optimized for touch input, allowing you to pin defects directly from your phone or tablet.
What file formats are supported for 3D scans? expand_more
The platform supports GLB (GL Transmission Binary) files for 3D models. You can upload your scans in this format via the Lidar Scans page. PDF reports are also supported for documentation.
How do I assign a lawyer to my case? expand_more
From the Reporting Dashboard, select a registered lawyer from the dropdown list during the claim setup process. You must fill in all required claim details before the lawyer can begin reviewing your case.
What roles are available on the platform? expand_more
There are three main roles: Homeowner (report defects and manage projects), Developer (review and rectify reported defects), and Lawyer (review evidence, generate legal reports). Each role has a tailored dashboard.
Is my data secure? expand_more
Yes. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. User authentication is handled securely, and case information is only accessible to the involved parties. We follow industry best practices for data protection.
